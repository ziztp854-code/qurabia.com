# Mamluk world map

Geographic intelligence for Mamluk Wars. A player's map is an authorized observation of the world at a particular time.

## Language

**WorldMap**: A game world's identity and geographic reference system.

**Region**: A named geographic area grouping cities and territories.

**Territory**: A geographic area whose ownership is determined by the game authority.

**City**: A strategic settlement at a real geographic location.

**Castle**: A fortified strategic location, optionally associated with a city.

**ArmyPosition**: An army's location and movement status as determined by the game authority.
_Avoid_: Predicted position

**ArmyRoute**: An authoritative travel plan between geographic locations, including waypoints and its schedule.

**SultanateTerritory**: An explicitly observable area controlled by a sultanate.

**SiegeMarker**: An observable siege at a city or castle, with its authoritative status.

**VisibilityRegion**: A time-limited area of current vision granted to one player.

**Shared vision**: A directional, revocable grant of vision to another player. Alliance membership alone does not imply it.

**Fog of War**: The part of a player's current map for which no active vision is granted.
_Avoid_: Hidden browser data
